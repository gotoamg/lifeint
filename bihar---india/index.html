About Us

LIFE's Bihar Mission partners with The Duncan Hospital to provide opportunities in Obstetrics, Gynaecology, Neonatology, Paediatrics, Intensive Care, and Community Medicine. Located approximately four and a half hours from Patna, the mission offers on-campus accommodation at the retreat camp. Participants are required to obtain medical clearance from the National Medical Council, an approved NGO, or a recognized medical institution before serving. The Duncan Hospital was established in 1930.
