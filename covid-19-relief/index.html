Covid-19 Relief


India is currently facing a devastating second wave of COVID-19, with case numbers far exceeding those seen during the first surge. The healthcare system is overwhelmed, and there is a critical shortage of essential resources—especially oxygen. Many patients are unable to receive the oxygen support they need, and lives are being lost due to this shortage. Access to oxygen concentrators, which can deliver 5–10 liters per minute (LPM), is urgently needed to help sustain those affected.

In response to this crisis, Luke’s International Fellowship and Enrichment (LIFE) is partnering with Friends of Agape, based in Colorado Springs, Colorado, to help provide oxygen concentrators and meet these immediate needs. Through this partnership, we aim to bring timely relief and hope to those suffering.


At times, we may wonder how we can truly make a difference with what we have. Supporting this effort is a meaningful way to help. As Scripture reminds us:

"You are the light of the world—like a city on a hilltop that cannot be hidden. No one lights a lamp and then puts it under a basket. Instead, a lamp is placed on a stand, where it gives light to everyone in the house. In the same way, let your good deeds shine for all to see, so that everyone will praise your Heavenly Father." (Matthew 5:14–16)

Even a small act of generosity can have a profound impact. We ask that you prayerfully consider contributing to this cause, helping to alleviate suffering and bring hope to our brothers and sisters in India. Your support can be a light in this time of darkness.
