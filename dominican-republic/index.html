Details

Our medical mission in the Dominican Republic is carried out in partnership with Word of Life who have been faithfully serving the people of the Dominican Republic since 1994. Our purpose is to show the love of Christ and share the gospel by serving the communities through Primary Care, and Pediatric medicine as well as various medical specialty services. Team members work alongside the Word of Life team and are accommodated at the Word of Life Retreat Camp in the region. All medical activities are conducted with the required clearance from the Colegio Médico Dominicano, ensuring compliance with local regulations. Everyone who attends the camp meets personally with the evangelism team to hear the gospel and team members get to engage the community and share their personal testimonies.

About Us

Our Last Visit in DR

Our Dominican Republic Mission was a remarkable journey of faith, service, and learning. Throughout the outreach, healthcare professionals, students, and volunteers from different parts of the world came together with one purpose, to demonstrate the love of Christ through compassionate medical care.

Each day began with prayer, devotion, and team briefings before heading into clinics and community outreach. Working alongside local healthcare professionals and our ministry partners at Word of Life, the team served patients through Community Medicine, Primary Care, and Paediatric services, offering medical consultations, health education, and compassionate care to those in need.
