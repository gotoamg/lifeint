About Luke's International Fellowship & Enrichment - LIFE


We are a faith-based, non-profit network of Christian healthcare professionals dedicated to serving God and fulfilling the Great Commission. Rooted in Christian values, LIFE engages in medical missions, health education, community outreach, holistic well being and spiritual discipleship — bringing healing, hope, and transformation to individuals and communities worldwide.-

Our Mission

Our Mission


To share the gospel of Jesus Christ through healthcare. It aims to strengthen healthcare professionals in their walk with God, equip them with the skills of an Evangelist, and provide opportunities to be an effective witness of Jesus Christ.

Our Campaigns

Medical Missions

Local and overseas, bringing clinical services to the poor and undeserved in partnership with communities, hospitals and medical schools.

Spiritual Fellowship

Provide services that encourage, strengthen and foster a community of Christian healthcare professionals.

Medical Education

Mentoring through individual and group sessions, seminars for continuing medical education, scholarships and medical training opportunities both locally and internationally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Luke's International Fellowship & Enrichment - LIFE offer?

LIFE is a Christ-centered mission with a primary focus on medical outreach and compassionate care. We organize medical camps, health support initiatives, and humanitarian aid for underserved communities, while also providing spiritual encouragement and discipleship. Alongside healthcare outreach, we conduct Bible teaching, mentorship programs, and mission partnerships that bring both physical healing and spiritual hope. Our mission is to serve people holistically — body, mind, and spirit.

How can I get started?

You can get involved with LIFE by partnering with our medical outreach and mission programs. Volunteers, healthcare professionals, supporters, and prayer partners are all welcome. Simply reach out through our contact page to join an outreach team, sponsor a project, or participate in our fellowship and training programs. We’ll guide you in finding the best way to serve and make an impact.

What makes you different?

What makes LIFE unique is our integration of medical care and Christ-centered mission. We combine practical healthcare support with spiritual mentorship and discipleship. Our outreach is driven by compassion, faith, and long-term community impact — not just short-term aid. We aim to build relationships, restore dignity, and share hope through both healing service and the message of Christ.
