“Fully Funded Medical Mission to Kenya – Transform Lives While Growing as a Physician”

Serve. Learn. Impact.

This mission trip is open to medical students and residents passionate about service, faith, and global health. Tenwek Hospital is a leading healthcare institution in Kenya, widely recognized for its excellence in many specialities including critical care, ophthalmology, cardiothoracic, and orthopedic surgery.


Located approximately 4.5 hours from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport in Nairobi, the hospital provides a strategic setting for medical missions and clinical learning. Established in 1937, Tenwek Hospital has a long-standing legacy of delivering compassionate, Christ-centered healthcare while training and equipping healthcare professionals who serve all over Africa.


This scholarship will help pay for expenses and participants will be accommodated in on-campus facilities, ensuring a safe and convenient stay throughout the mission. Medical professionals participating in the program are expected to meet the registration requirements of the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council and will work with Samaritan's Purse for logistics.
