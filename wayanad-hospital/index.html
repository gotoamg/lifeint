About Us

Sharing the Love of Christ Through Care and Compassion

Mission Wayanad united LIFE and ICPF in a shared calling to bring the love of Christ to the people of Wayanad, India through compassionate medical care, practical service, and the hope of the Gospel.


Physicians, healthcare professionals, students, and ministry volunteers served side by side, providing medical consultations, patient care, health education, prayer, worship, and fellowship. Together, they met both physical needs and eternal needs with compassion and excellence.


The impact extended far beyond medicine. Lives were encouraged, families were cared for, and hearts were transformed. Through the faithful witness of the team, many experienced the love of Christ, and several made the life-changing decision to accept Jesus Christ as their personal Savior.


Our medical mission is conducted in partnership with Wayanad Community Hospital, established in 2022, specializing in Community Medicine, Primary Care, and Paediatrics. Accommodation is provided at our on-premises retreat camp. All participants must have valid medical clearance from a National Medical Council, an approved NGO, or a recognized medical institution. The mission is coordinated by Dr. Tony Joseph and Dr. Muralidar.
