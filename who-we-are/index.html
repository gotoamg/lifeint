About Us

Luke’s International Fellowship and Enrichment, Inc is a nonprofit corporation of Christian health care professionals that is organized exclusively for religious, educational, and charitable purposes as defined under 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. The purpose of this organization is to bring healing to the body and Spirit by delivering medical care and sharing the good news of the gospel of Jesus Christ. We seek to accomplish these ends by mobilizing resources and medical expertise both in the United States and around the world with a special focus on the South Asian subcontinent.The objectives of this corporation are to foster the spiritual growth of Christian health care professionals, promote the development of health care professionals to be effective witnesses of Jesus Christ, conduct and support short and long term medical missions both locally and internationally, provide continuing medical education for healthcare medical professionals germane to the objectives of this corporation,promote public health efforts directly and by dissemination of medical literature both independently and in collaboration with other like-minded institutions, provide networking opportunities for like-minded individuals to greater fulfill the objectives of LIFE.The individual and summative effect of these objectives will be to improve the health and health awareness of those we serve and to fulfill the Great Commission as directed by Jesus Christ.

We are a faith-based, non-profit network of Christian healthcare professionals dedicated to serving God and fulfilling the Great Commission.
